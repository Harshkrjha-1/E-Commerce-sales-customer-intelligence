# Executive Business Recommendations Report

## 1. High-Value At-Risk Customer Retention
- **Issue**: 28% of total customer base are classified as 'At Risk' or 'High Value At Risk'.
- **Evidence**: Historical revenue from at-risk accounts exceeds R$ 4.5M. Recency exceeds 180 days with zero repeat purchase activity.
- **Action**: Implement personalized re-engagement campaigns with tiered 20% discount vouchers.
- **Objective**: Recover high-LTV revenue and increase repeat purchase rate by 3-5%.

## 2. Regional Delivery SLA Optimization
- **Issue**: States including RJ, BA, and AL experience delivery delay rates > 10%.
- **Evidence**: Orders in regional states take 20+ days lead time compared to 8.5 days in SP. Review scores drop from 4.3 to 2.1 on delayed orders.
- **Action**: Partner with local regional carriers and set up distribution hubs in northeastern state hubs.
- **Objective**: Reduce regional delivery latency by 25% and bring delay rates below 5%.

## 3. Marketplace Seller SLA Enforcement
- **Issue**: Active merchants with high delay rates drive down platform review scores.
- **Evidence**: 3,088 sellers evaluated; top delay offenders lower buyer satisfaction scores.
- **Action**: Enforce strict 48-hour dispatch SLA windows and apply buy-box suppression for repeat offenders.
- **Objective**: Elevate overall marketplace review ratings above 4.5 stars.
